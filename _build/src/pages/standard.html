Quality

Quality starts at the source.

1CELL works with production under cGMP conditions, and every lot is released against its specification before it moves.

  • cGMP production with documented procedures
  • Release against specification, lot by lot
  • Sterility and endotoxin testing, where applicable
  • Viability thresholds for cell products
Laboratory
Testing

What is tested, by product type.

Exact tests and acceptance criteria are listed on each lot's Certificate of Analysis.

Cell therapies

MSC · NK · Muse

  • Identity and purity markers (flow cytometry)
  • Viability
  • Sterility and endotoxin
  • Cell count per dose

Exosomes

Extracellular vesicles

  • Particle concentration and size (NTA)
  • Tetraspanins CD9, CD63, CD81
  • Sterility and endotoxin, where applicable

Peptides

1CELL Longevity

  • Purity by HPLC
  • Identity by mass spectrometry
  • Endotoxin
Traceability
