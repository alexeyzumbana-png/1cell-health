What it is

Defined by what they express — and what they don't.

Mesenchymal stem (stromal) cells are multipotent cells with differentiation and paracrine signaling capacity. 1CELL's MSCs are expanded under cGMP culture with controlled passages and characterized by multicolor flow cytometry before release.

Each lot is released against specification and supplied with a lot-specific Certificate of Analysis.

Scientific context

What the research says.

MSCs are among the most widely studied cell types in regenerative medicine. Much of their observed activity is attributed to paracrine and immunomodulatory signaling. Clinical studies span orthopedic, inflammatory and immune-mediated conditions, with results that vary by indication and protocol.

Regulatory status differs by country and indication; many applications remain investigational.
