What it is

A defined subpopulation.

Muse (Multilineage-differentiating Stress-Enduring) cells are a stress-tolerant subpopulation found within mesenchymal tissues, identified by SSEA-3 expression.

1CELL supplies SSEA-3+ Muse cells in a standardized per-dose presentation for consistent, repeatable application, with a lot-specific Certificate of Analysis.

Scientific context

What the research says.

Muse cells were first described in 2010. Preclinical research describes multilineage differentiation and migration toward damaged tissue. Early clinical studies have been reported, mostly in Japan.

Evidence in humans is still limited; Muse cell therapies should be considered investigational.

FAQ

Muse cell questions.

How do they differ from MSCs?

Muse cells are a specific SSEA-3+ subpopulation found within mesenchymal tissues. They are characterized and supplied separately from MSCs.

Who can order them?

Licensed physicians and clinics, where permitted by local regulation. Request professional access and we will verify your credentials.

How are they shipped?

Under the conditions specified for the product — cryogenic or controlled cold chain — with temperature monitoring and a custody record.

Are Muse cell therapies approved?

Human evidence is still limited and they should be considered investigational. Use is the treating physician's decision within applicable regulation.
