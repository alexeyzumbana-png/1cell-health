What ships with each product?

Every lot ships with a third-party Certificate of Analysis, flow-cytometry characterization and a clinical protocol covering dosage, route and follow-up. Cellular therapies also include reported viability for the batch.

How is the cold chain guaranteed?

Shipments are held at 2–8 °C and carry a digital temperature logger monitored 24/7. If a reading falls out of range, the lot is automatically rejected before it reaches a patient.

Who can order professional products?

Cellular therapies and exosomes are available to licensed physicians and clinics. Professional access is granted after license verification — typically within 24 hours — and unlocks institutional pricing and clinical protocols.

Can individuals buy directly?

Yes. Peptides, nutraceuticals and cosmetics are available for direct purchase and ship in cold chain with a protocol included. Consult a qualified clinician before use.
