Questions

The essentials.

All questions→
What does 1CELL Health do?

We supply advanced regenerative products to physicians and clinics and coordinate the specialized logistics they require — preservation, cold-chain or cryogenic transport, documentation and delivery.

Is everything shipped at 2–8 °C?

No. Conditions depend on the product: some require controlled refrigeration, others frozen or cryogenic storage. We confirm the requirements with the source laboratory and coordinate packaging and transport accordingly.

Who can order clinical products?

Cell therapies and exosomes are supplied to licensed physicians and clinics, where permitted by local regulation. We verify professional credentials before granting access.

What documentation comes with a product?

Each lot is released with a lot-specific Certificate of Analysis. Depending on the product, characterization reports and custody records are also available to verified professionals.
